# Live Preview

The **Live Preview** at the top of the **Designs** tab draws the design being edited against sample artwork, in **Landscape** and **Portrait** side by side, and draws it again as the settings change. **Preview As** picks what it shows, **Series**, **Season**, **Episode**, **Movie**, **Video**, **Collection**, or **Folder**. The **Logos** tab previews a logo design the same way, against the name typed in **Sample Name**, over a **Dark**, **Light**, or **Checkered** background. Select a preview to enlarge it.
